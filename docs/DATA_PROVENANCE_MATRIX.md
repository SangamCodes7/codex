# Data Provenance Matrix (Mumbai V1 & Delhi V2)

## 1. Governance Principles & Strict Provenance Rules

Scientific integrity is the cornerstone of the SIH 2026 Urban Flood Nowcasting System. To prevent misleading emergency managers or fabricating accuracy:
1. **Never conflate Model Output with Observed Ground Truth**: Modelled inundation depths ($d_{\text{model}}$) are derived hypotheses, whereas field observations ($d_{\text{obs}}$) are empirical benchmarks used strictly for evaluation.
2. **Never claim forecast is Radar Observation**: Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) forecasts (e.g. Open-Meteo) must be explicitly stamped with `NWP_FORECAST` and never masquerade as radar.
3. **Never color-decode uncalibrated Radar GIFs**: IMD radar imagery without official quantitative precipitation estimation (QPE) binary grids will remain uningested as quantitative input.
4. **Never claim 30m DEM is 10m/5m**: Resampling a 30m grid does not create higher spatial resolution.
5. **Never invent unmeasured invert levels**: Drainage bed elevations sampled from surface DEMs must be tagged `DERIVED`, not `OBSERVED`.
