## 1. Executive Summary

A thorough investigation of official municipal engineering archives, e-procurement portals, notice inviting tenders (NITs), and inter-agency filings was conducted to determine whether official engineering records (longitudinal sections, cross-sections, structural drawings, and topographical surveys) can supply the missing hydraulic geometry for the Kushak Nallah corridor.

### Key Audit Conclusions:
1. **Engineering Records Confirmed to Exist**:
   - Official engineering documentation—including longitudinal sections, cross-sections, invert profile levels, and structural as-built drawings—is explicitly confirmed to exist.
   - NDMC Notice Inviting Tender **NIT No. 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26** (Tender ID: `2026_NDMC_297503_1`, Value: ₹12.30 Crore) for *"Desilting of RCC Covered Kushak Nallah and Ring Road Nallah"* formally states that longitudinal sections, cross-sections, invert profile levels, and detailed structural drawings are actively maintained in the office of the Executive Engineer (Roads-III).
   - NDMC commissioned a detailed topographical survey of Kushak Nallah from S.P. Marg to Satya Sadan / Lodhi Colony (around 2020).
2. **Public Availability Barrier**:
   - While tender notices, NITs, and administrative work scopes are published on the Delhi Government e-procurement portal (`govtprocurement.delhi.gov.in`), the **actual numeric engineering drawings, survey CAD deliverables (.dwg), and invert schedules are NOT publicly downloadable**.
   - Standard municipal contracting practice requires bidders and engineers to physically inspect structural drawings and longitudinal profiles in the office of the **Executive Engineer (R-III), Room No. 306, SBS Place, Gole Market, New Delhi**.
   - The 2020 NDMC topographical survey output was never released to the public domain.
3. **Engineering Verdict**:
   - **Overall Status**: `OFFICIAL BUT ACCESS-RESTRICTED`.
   - **Public Web Recovery**: **NOT PUBLICLY RECOVERABLE**.
   - **Field Survey Requirement**: A targeted **FIELD SURVEY REMAINS REQUIRED** for independent public-domain modeling, unless formal institutional access to the NDMC R-III engineering archives is obtained.

## 2. Forensic Audit of the Five P0 Hydraulic Geometry Gaps

### P0-1: Kushak Longitudinal Invert Profile
- **Gap Description**: Continuous bed/invert elevation profile along the 5.028 km Kushak Nallah corridor from S.P. Marg down to the Barapullah confluence.
- **Potential Official Document**:
  - NDMC R-III Division Kushak Nallah L-Section Engineering Profile.
  - NDMC 2020 Topographical Survey of Kushak Nallah (S.P. Marg to Satya Sadan / Lodhi Colony).
- **Responsible Agency**: New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC) Civil Engineering Department (Roads-III Division).
- **Evidence of Existence**:
  - Formally cited in active tender documentation: `NIT 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26` explicitly notes that invert profile levels and longitudinal sections are maintained in the R-III office for the Kushak covered reach.
- **Is Numeric Geometry Publicly Downloadable?**: **NO**.
- **Access Status**: **`OFFICIAL BUT ACCESS-RESTRICTED`**.
- **Exact Document / Archive Reference**:
  - Archive Location: Office of Executive Engineer (R-III), Room No. 306, 3rd Floor, SBS Place, Gole Market, New Delhi - 110001.
  - Tender Identifier: `NIT 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26` / `Tender ID: 2026_NDMC_297503_1` (`govtprocurement.delhi.gov.in`).
- **What Exact Geometry it Contains (in office)**: Design bed elevation, invert slope gradients, step-drops at culvert transitions, and manhole invert levels.
- **Can it Replace a Field Survey?**: **PARTIAL / CONDITIONAL**. While it provides design invert levels, decades of heavy siltation (frequently 1.0–2.0 m deep as documented in NGT OA 06/2012 proceedings) mean current operational bed levels diverge significantly from design inverts. Physical ground-truthing is still required.

### P0-2: Kushak Cross-Sections
- **Gap Description**: Transverse channel geometry (bed width, bank slope, top width, wall thickness) at regular intervals along the corridor.
- **Potential Official Document**:
  - NDMC Kushak Cross-Sectional Survey Sheets (2020 Topographical Survey).
  - IIT Delhi 2018 Drainage Master Plan Barapullah Basin Survey Sheets (Section 3.2 surveyed 601 cross-sections across Delhi, but individual raw Kushak station sheets were withheld from the public summary).
- **Responsible Agency**: NDMC (Roads-III) / IIT Delhi / Delhi I&FC.
- **Evidence of Existence**:
  - Referenced in NDMC NIT `52/EE(R-III)/2025-26`.
  - Confirmed in IIT Delhi 2018 Master Plan report (fieldwork executed by DGPS/Total Station survey teams).
- **Is Numeric Geometry Publicly Downloadable?**: **NO**.
  - In GSDL GIS: Only nominal administrative attributes exist (`Bed Width = 25.0 m`, `Depth = 7.35 m`), which Phase 3B disproved as hydraulic clear geometry.
  - Public IIT Delhi reports provide only generalized basin summaries and representative schematic figures, omitting tabular coordinate files.
- **Access Status**: **`OFFICIAL BUT ACCESS-RESTRICTED`**.
- **Exact Document / Archive Reference**:
  - NDMC Civil Engineering Department Drawing Archive, SBS Place.
  - IIT Delhi 2018 Drainage Master Plan — Volume II: Basin Survey Data (Unpublished institutional annexure).
- **What Exact Geometry it Contains (in office)**: Transverse chainage-elevation coordinates, masonry/RCC wall dimensions, bank crest levels.
- **Can it Replace a Field Survey?**: **PARTIAL**. Structural masonry wall dimensions are valid, but active cross-sectional silt profiles require field survey.

### P0-3: Africa Avenue Underground Conduit Clear Width/Height
- **Gap Description**: Internal clear dimensions (number of barrels/cells, clear span $W$, clear rise $H$) of the covered Kushak box culvert running beneath Africa Avenue / Brigadier Hoshiyar Singh Marg.
- **Potential Official Document**:
  - NDMC As-Built Structural Drawings for Covered Kushak Drain (Africa Avenue reach).
  - JNNURM / PWD Covered Drain Project Completion Drawings.
  - UTTIPEC Traffic & Drainage Integration Drawings for Africa Avenue.
- **Responsible Agency**: NDMC (Civil Engineering Department / Roads-III) & UTTIPEC / PWD.
- **Evidence of Existence**:
  - NDMC Council budget statement V and civil department records document the construction and rehabilitation of the RCC covered box culvert along Africa Avenue ("egg-shaped barrels", multi-cell RCC box).
  - Delhi High Court filings (WP(C) 1934/2004, 2006, 2010) confirm the drain was covered under sanctioned municipal engineering drawings.
- **Is Numeric Geometry Publicly Downloadable?**: **NO**.
  - Public budget documents, council minutes, and court affidavits mention the project scope and expenditure, but do not provide the detailed structural as-built shop drawings.
- **Access Status**: **`OFFICIAL BUT ACCESS-RESTRICTED`**.
- **Exact Document / Archive Reference**:
  - NDMC Civil Engineering Dept — As-Built Structural Drawings of RCC Box Culvert over Kushak Nallah at Africa Avenue.
- **What Exact Geometry it Contains (in office)**: Clear opening width per barrel ($W$), vertical opening clearance ($H$), number of cells, deck slab thickness.
- **Can it Replace a Field Survey?**: **YES (for structural geometry)**. As-built structural drawings are the definitive engineering authority for reinforced concrete culverts (confined subterranean spaces where physical total-station survey is hazardous). However, effective clear area depends on current silt depth.

## 3. Investigation of the 2020 NDMC Topographical Survey Output

A specific investigation was conducted into the status of the **2020 NDMC Topographical Survey of Kushak Nallah**:

1. **Procurement vs. Output Analysis**:
   - The tender for *"Conducting of Detailed Topographical Survey of Kushak Nallah from S.P. Marg to Satya Sadan / Lodhi Colony"* was commissioned as an engineering service contract.
   - Public procurement notices exist on tender aggregation and e-procurement systems (`govtprocurement.delhi.gov.in`, `tendersinfo.net`, `tenderkart.in`).
2. **Deliverable Dissemination**:
   - The contract deliverables (AutoCAD `.dwg` files, digital elevation models, cross-section coordinate tables, and longitudinal profile sheets) are delivered directly to the municipal division (R-III) on storage media.
   - Municipal agencies in Delhi do not publish raw survey CAD files or point clouds on open web portals due to security and proprietary contracting rules.
3. **Audit Finding**:
   - **The 2020 NDMC topographical survey OUTPUT itself is NOT publicly accessible**.
   - It is an existing, verified internal technical record held in the physical archives of the NDMC Civil Engineering Department.
