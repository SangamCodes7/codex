# DELHI KUSHAK — OPERATIONAL TIMELINE 2015–2026

Chronological register of documented operational changes/affecting events for the Kushak system (western/NDMC spine + southern collector). Every entry carries its evidence ID from `kushak_operational_evidence.csv`. Entries marked **[K]** name a Kushak reach or agency jurisdiction over one. Entries without [K] are city-wide/adjacent context.

Format: DATE — CHANGE/EVENT — EVIDENCE — CLASS.

---

## Pre-2015 baseline (context)

- **2007** — NDMC Sub-city Plan: Africa Avenue railway underpass documented as a pumped node ("accumulated water has to be pumped out"); lagoon-retention project (8 lagoons, 2 h retention) proposed S.P. Marg→Satya Marg. — OP-STR-001, OP-STR-009 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (plan)
- **2011** — CGWB Kushak Nala pilot: 2 gabion + 2 nala bunds constructed in headwaters (110,000 m³ recharge). — OP-STR-007 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL
- **~2013–2014** — JNNURM-funded covering/parking conversion of Kushak Drain stretches; NGT grievance filed (OA 300/2013). — OP-STR-006 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL

## 2015

- **13-01-2015** — NGT judgment (OA 6/2012 & 300/2013): covering restrained; iron rods ordered removed from four named reaches (Pushp Vihar, Chirag Delhi, GK-I, Andrews Ganj). **[K-southern]** — OP-STR-006 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL

## 2017

- **Monsoon 2017** — PWD Flood Control Order 2017 in force: city-wide underpass pump inventories (pp.58-73), I&FC regulator list (p.86), DJB SPS list (p.87). **[K-adjacent: Moolchand/AIIMS underpasses in zone lists]** — OP-PUMP-005, OP-STR-012 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (Kushak rows pending extraction)
- **23-08-2017** — Revenue-department register row: "Africa Avenue Road Under Hyatt Flyover" flood entry. **[K]** — snapshot `revenue_directory_fco2023_snippet.txt` — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (MEDIUM; OCR-level detail)

## 2018

- **Jul 2018** — IIT Delhi DMP-2018 delivered: regulators/gates/weirs/portable pumps **excluded from modelling** for lack of operational rules; 25.9% adverse-slope conduits in Barapullah basin; Moolchand/AIIMS/Defence Colony underpasses identified as pumped low points. — OP-STR-011 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL

## 2021

- **23-09-2021** — Press: two 500 HP pumps installed at Moolchand underpass; 800 HP pump procured for Pul Prahladpur. **[K-adjacent]** — OP-PUMP-006 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (MEDIUM)
- **Budget cycle 2021-22** — NDMC budget line: submersible pump for under-bridge Africa Avenue (₹150 lakh RE). **[K]** — OP-PUMP-003 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (provision only; commissioning UNKNOWN)

## 2022

- **Aug 2022** — NMCG Delhi MPR: "trapping flow in **Kushak drain at Andrews Ganj Pumping station** have been completed"; Kushak Nala (NDMC stretch) under bio-remediation. **[K-southern + NDMC]** — OP-PUMP-008, OP-STR-010 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL — *DWF regime change on the southern collector.*
- **Oct–Nov 2022** — Najafgarh flagship desilting (17,000 MT, 2 km) — context only, different system. — OP-DES-006 — BACKGROUND

## 2023

- **08–13 Jul 2023** — Multi-day pluvial events (corridor responses 8–9 Jul per DTP) followed by compound Yamuna flood (ORB peak 208.66 m on 13 Jul; regulator pump-out doctrine applicable per FCO framework). No corridor pump/maintenance records located. — OP-STR-013 (doctrine) — operational silence documented

## 2024

- **14-06-2024** — I&FC: 10 of 15 lakh tonnes silt collected city-wide; target 15 by 15-06. — OP-DES-005 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (MEDIUM)
- **28-06-2024 (EVT-2024-06-28)** — LG Delhi, same day: Flood Control Order + de-silting "yet pending" past 15-06. CWC: free-outfall boundary 27–30 Jun. — OP-PUMP-010, OP-NEG-004 — maintenance state on event day OFFICIALLY DISPUTED
- **26–27-07-2024 (EVT-2024-07-26)** — Africa Avenue underpass flooded to 6-7 ft; NDMC pumped with extra resources; pumps "functioned efficiently" but inflow overwhelmed pipes; clogging from construction material suspected; NR construction diversion alleged; NR alleges absent area drains + leaking NDMC/DJB pipelines. Fleet: 120 fixed + 62 portable pumps; Africa Avenue on 26-point vulnerable list. **[K]** — OP-PUMP-001/002, OP-STR-005, OP-EVT-001 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL, HIGH — *strongest event-conditioned operational evidence.*
- **~Aug–Sep 2024** — LG/CS intervention triggers cleaning of Dayal Singh College, Lodhi Road, DTC Depot reaches after "severe flooding". **[K]** — OP-DES-001 (retrospective statement, Mar-2025) — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (MEDIUM; completion date unverified)

## 2025

- **05-03 / 09-04-2025** — NGT Joint Inspection: depot reach silt 1.5-3 ft in all 5 bays; flow ~1 ft through 2/5 bays at DWF; openings 1.5×1.5 m @50 m; chambers 2.5×1.15 m @100 m. **[K]** — OP-DES-009, OP-STR-003, OP-NEG-003 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL, HIGH — *pre-intervention state anchor.*
- **19-03-2025** — NDMC monsoon plan: "limited drain capacity (25 mm per hour)", "inverse gradient issues at key outfalls such as Sunheri Pulla Nallah and Purana Qila Road"; tender floated for desilting at **Dayal Singh College, Lodhi Road, DTC Depot** (award Apr-2025, completion Jun-2025); 8 new 32 HP trolley-mounted pumps acquired. **[K]** — OP-STR-002, OP-DES-001, OP-PUMP-004 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL, HIGH
- **Feb 2025** — Moolchand underpass pump upgradation inspected by minister. **[K-adjacent]** — OP-PUMP-007 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (MEDIUM)
- **22-05-2025** — I&FC: 66% of covered sections desilted; 21,252 MT target. — OP-DES-003 — DERIVED (MEDIUM)
- **29-05-2025** — Sunehri Pul confluence geometry (5×10 m boxes) via tender-press. — OP-STR-008 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL (MEDIUM)
- **2025-26 (NIT)** — NIT 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26: desilting of RCC covered Kushak Nallah + Ring Road Nallah (21,406 m³; robotic/super-sucker; flow blocking required; dewatering pumps integral). **[K]** — OP-DES-002 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL — *method documents artificial flow control during works.*
- **07-07 / 20-08-2025** — NGT directs MCD to expedite de-silting; wire mesh at upstream of Defence Colony drain to be installed from 15-09-2025 with anti-clogging O&M; DJB proposes augmented Andrews Ganj SPS. **[K-southern]** — OP-DES-008, OP-STR-004, OP-PUMP-009 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL, HIGH

## 2026

- **28-01-2026** — HC CONT.CAS(C) 434/2022 status: MCD→I&FC as-is-where-is handovers of Kushak reaches documented (1.75 km + 1.0 km depot + 0.87 km INA→LLRM). **[K]** — (Phase 7D-15 G5, court record) — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL — *jurisdictional/operational responsibility transfer.*
- **25–26-08-2026 (EVT-2026-08-25)** — ITO: 6 PTO + 14 permanent pumps + 15 personnel deployed; "temporary accumulation" framing. **[adjacent mainstem]** — OP-PUMP-010b, OP-NEG-001 — OBSERVED_OFFICIAL, HIGH

## Summary of regime changes relevant to event interpretation

| Window | Regime state (documented) |
|---|---|
| 2015 → mid-2022 | Covered/parking-converted stretches under judicial restraint; no Kushak storm-pump records; southern collector carries raw sewage inflows |
| Aug 2022 | Andrews Ganj SPS trapping completed → southern-spine DWF regime changes |
| Monsoon 2024 | De-silting completion officially disputed on EV-2024-06-28 day; Africa Ave underpass pump chain overwhelmed 26-07-2024 amid alleged construction interference |
| Mar–Jun 2025 | JIR documents heavy silt + 2/5-bay conveyance; NDMC desilting tender (depot reach) targeted for completion by Jun-2025; NIT-52 (Africa Ave box) issued 2025-26 |
| Sep 2025 → | Wire-mesh screen on Defence Colony upstream; augmented Andrews Ganj SPS proposed; MCD→I&FC handovers formalized Jan-2026 |

**Reading rule**: no catalogue event (2021, 2023, 2024) has a documented *post*-maintenance state observation, and no event has pump-operation records on the western spine except EV-2024-07-26. Any hydraulic comparison across events inherits these regime differences; none may be normalized away.
