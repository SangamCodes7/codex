**Subject:** Request for Information under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 for **existing engineering drawings, longitudinal sections, cross-sections, and structural invert profiles of Kushak Nallah** associated with **NIT No. 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26** and **NIT No. 51/EE(R-III)/2020-21**.

**Sir/Madam,**

Kindly provide certified, legible copies of the following **existing official engineering records and survey deliverables** maintained in the archives or drawing registers of the Executive Engineer (Road-III) relating to the covered Kushak Nallah (including sections along Sardar Patel Marg, Nehru Park, Africa Avenue, Brig Hoshiar Singh Marg, Satya Sadan, and Lodhi Colony):

### PART A — Existing As-Built Structural & Hydraulic Drawings
1. **Longitudinal Sections (L-Sections):** As-built longitudinal section profile drawings of the covered Kushak Nallah showing continuous bed/invert levels, ground/road levels, and internal soffit (slab bottom) levels against chainage.
2. **Cross-Sectional Drawings:** Detailed structural cross-sections of the covered RCC box conduit showing:
   - Internal clear barrel width and height.
   - Number of cells/bays (e.g., single-cell, twin-cell, or multi-cell sections).
   - Internal dividing wall and outer wall thicknesses.
   - Foundation slab and top slab structural thicknesses.
3. **Inlet & Outlet Structures:** Engineering drawings of transition sections where open channels transition into covered conduit barrels (such as upstream of Africa Avenue and near Kamal Ataturk Marg).
4. **Intermediate Access & Silt Chambers:** Plan and sectional drawings showing spacing, chainage, and opening dimensions of intermediate manholes, access shafts (e.g., 1.5 m × 1.5 m), and silt chambers (e.g., 2.50 m × 1.15 m).
5. **Bridge & Culvert Crossings:** As-built structural opening drawings and invert/soffit elevations of road culverts and crossings along the Kushak corridor within R-III jurisdiction.
6. **Lateral Inflow Connections:** Structural and hydraulic connection drawings showing the locations, invert levels, and dimensions of municipal storm sewer pipes and feeder drains discharging laterally into Kushak Nallah.

### PART B — Predecessor & Baseline Survey Deliverables (NIT 51/2020-21 & NIT 52/2025-26)
7. **2020 Topographical Survey Report:** A complete copy of the survey report, drawing sheets, and deliverables resulting from **NIT No. 51/EE(R-III)/2020-21** (*"Conducting Of Detailed Topographical Survey Of Kushak Nallah From S.P Marg To Satya Sadan / Lodhi Colony"*).
8. **Survey Control & Benchmark Records:** Official records identifying the vertical benchmark (GTS or local NDMC reference mark) and datum used for bed level reductions along Kushak Nallah.
9. **Drawings Referenced in NIT-52:** Copies of all standard, structural, or baseline drawings available for bidder inspection in Room 306, SBS Place as referenced under the general conditions of **NIT No. 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26**.
10. **Invert Profile Level Register:** Excerpt of the official Invert Level Register / Measurement Book (MB) recordings establishing the nominal bed slope and invert levels of the covered barrel.

### PART C — Preferred Mode of Access & Format
11. **Digital CAD Files:** For CAD drawings, the applicant requests native electronic vector copies (.dwg or .dxf) on CD/DVD/USB drive pursuant to Section 7(9) of the RTI Act, 2005.
12. **Physical Record Inspection:** If electronic reproduction of large drawing rolls (A0/A1 size) or historical registers is difficult, the applicant formally requests permission to **inspect the original drawings, roll plans, and survey books in Room No. 306, SBS Place** under Section 2(j)(i) of the RTI Act, 2005 at a mutually convenient date and time.
