## 7. What the Documents Establish

1. **Formal Statutory Recognition**: Kushak Nalla was officially cataloged by the Delhi Administration Flood Control Wing as the primary arterial drain of NDMC (`NDMC Drain #1`).
2. **Active Operational Reality**: Its official status was `"Existing /Remodeling"`, refuting any notion that it was an abandoned, obsolete, or purely conceptual channel.
3. **Physical Traceability**: It was verified as physically traceable, distinguishing it from drains that were obliterated or paved over without records (such as `karbala Drain` or `Hameedpur drain`).
4. **Complete Traceability of South Zone Arterials**: All major South Delhi drains connected to the Barapullah basin (Chirag Delhi, Naoroji Nagar, Sunehripulla, Lajpat Nagar, AIIMS) were active and traceable.
