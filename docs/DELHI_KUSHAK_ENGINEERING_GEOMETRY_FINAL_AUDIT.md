8. **Scope Enforcement**: Zero application code modified; zero SWMM conduits constructed; zero hydraulic equations solved; Mumbai V1 completely untouched.

### Critical Scientific Distinctions Established:
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **1. Structural Deck Width vs. Hydraulic Channel Width**:  
> The **50 m width** established in NGT and UTTIPEC records is the **surface deck width** of the reinforced concrete slab constructed for the Delhi Transport Corporation (DTC) Kushak Bus Depot. It is **NOT** the hydraulic clear waterway width of Kushak Nallah. The underlying active drainage waterway is constrained within narrower retaining walls beneath the deck.
>
> **2. Structural Column Grid vs. Hydraulic Clear Opening**:  
> The **4 m × 4 m grid** established in NGT records denotes structural column and beam spacing supporting the deck. The clear hydraulic opening between column faces is strictly less than 4.0 m (reduced by column width, typically 0.5–0.8 m). Furthermore, the dense matrix of vertical piers introduces substantial form drag, turbulence, and debris trapping, reducing effective hydraulic conveyance.

* **Hydraulic Implication**: Corroborates that municipal authorities manage a continuous monitored urban drain trunk of 3.85 km from the Ring Road daylight point down to the downstream rail/road bridges.
* **Evidence Class**: `OFFICIAL_INSPECTION` (High Confidence).

#### B. Kushak Bus Depot Deck Length (1,050 m) and Deck Width (50 m)
* **Document Citation**: NGT Order dt. 23.04.2025, Paragraph 4 and Joint Committee Inspection Table, Item 3.
* **Exact Wording / Paraphrase**:
  > *"The covered portion of the drain at the Kushak Bus Depot has a length of 1,050 meters and a width of 50 meters and is in the possession of the Delhi Transport Corporation (DTC), being utilized as a bus depot."*
* **Hydraulic vs. Structural Reality**:
  - The **50 m width** is the **surface deck slab** utilized for parking DTC low-floor and cluster buses.
  - The actual hydraulic clear width of the underlying stormwater channel is **NOT** stated in the report and remains **UNKNOWN**.
* **Evidence Class**: `OFFICIAL_AS_BUILT` (Deck Dimensions); `UNKNOWN` (Hydraulic Clear Waterway).

#### C. Substructure Column and Beam Matrix: 4.0 m × 4.0 m Grid
* **Document Citation**: NGT Order dt. 23.04.2025, Paragraph 6 (Report of the High Powered Committee on Desilting of Drains).
* **Exact Wording / Paraphrase**:
  > *"The covering of the drain was executed using RCC slabs resting on a framed structure composed of columns and beams at regular intervals of 4 meters x 4 meters. The dense placement of these columns and beams under the covered slab creates a severe impediment to maintenance and desilting operations, restricting the movement of machinery and trapping solid waste and silt underneath the deck."*
* **Hydraulic Significance**:
  - Confirms a severe structural constriction: hundreds of vertical RCC piers stand directly in the high-velocity flow path.
  - While clear centerline spacing is 4.0 m, the clear width between column faces is approximately $4.0 - 0.6 = 3.4\text{ m}$.
  - The pier grid creates massive eddy formation, bed shear disruption, and debris blockages, elevating effective channel roughness from $n=0.025$ to $n \ge 0.045$.
  - Clear vertical headroom and exact pier dimensions are **NOT** provided in the judicial record and remain **UNKNOWN**.
* **Evidence Class**: `OFFICIAL_AS_BUILT` (Grid Spacing); `UNKNOWN` (Clear Waterway Opening).

#### D. INA Market Constriction Bottleneck: 600 m
* **Document Citation**: NGT Order dt. 06.08.2024, Paragraph 4(ii) (MCD Status Report on Critical Drainage Chokepoints).
* **Exact Wording / Paraphrase**:
  > *"A critical pain point exists in a span length of 600 meters opposite INA Market where the Kushak drain is inaccessible for heavy machinery due to the presence of jhuggis on the bank, low clearance under the elevated road structure, and boundary walls constructed by the PWD."*
* **Hydraulic Significance**: Pinpoints a physical hydraulic throttle between Chainage ~3350m and ~3950m where vertical and lateral clearance is constricted by bridge viaducts and informal settlements.
* **Evidence Class**: `OFFICIAL_INSPECTION` (Very High Confidence).

#### E. Defence Colony Tributary Drain: 1,600 m (1,300 m Covered + 300 m Open)
* **Document Citation**: NGT Order dt. 23.04.2025, Paragraph 7 (Affidavit of MCD on South Delhi Storm Water Outfalls).
* **Exact Wording / Paraphrase**:
  > *"The Defence Colony drain, which is a major tributary discharging into the Kushak drain upstream of the Barapullah confluence, has a total length of approximately 1,600 meters. Of this total length, approximately 300 meters is an open drain and 1,300 meters is covered with an RCC roof structure resting on columns and beams."*
* **Hydraulic Status**: Confirms the physical layout of the primary eastern lateral tributary. However, cross-sectional dimensions, bed inverts, and inflow hydrographs are **NOT** published.
* **Evidence Class**: `OFFICIAL_INSPECTION` (Reach Segmentation); `UNKNOWN` (Hydraulic Dimensions).

## 10. Explicit Answers to the 8 Required Forensic Questions

### Question 1: Can CS-01–CS-07 now be replaced by measured sections?
> **NO.**  
> The newly discovered public primary sources (NGT OA 6/2012, UTTIPEC Project 35, NDMC NIT 52) provide macroscopic reach-level lengths and structural parking deck dimensions, but **zero surveyed channel cross-sections**. CS-01 through CS-07 must strictly remain classified as **`ASSUMED / REGULARIZED`** synthetic transects bounded by the verified $6—18\text{ m}$ planning width envelope.

### Question 2: Can the 2.318 km Africa Avenue section now be numerically modeled?
> **NO (Not deterministically using physically-based 1D/2D hydraulic equations).**  
> While its horizontal centerline alignment ($2,318.42\text{ m}$) and physical construction as an RCC covered box culvert are officially confirmed by NDMC NIT 52 and OSM Way 44351567, its **internal clear width, clear height, barrel cell count (single vs twin vs triple box), wall thickness, invert levels, and longitudinal bed slope are strictly UNKNOWN**. Any numerical modeling of this section would require completely synthetic assumptions regarding conduit cross-sectional area and slope.

### Question 3: Can the 1.05 km Bus Depot section now be numerically modeled?
> **PARTIALLY AS A MACRO FLOW-RESISTANCE ELEMENT, BUT NO FOR DETAILED CONDUIT / 1D-SAINT-VENANT MODELING WITHOUT REGULARIZED ASSUMPTIONS.**  
> The forensic audit firmly establishes the external structural dimensions ($1,050\text{ m}$ length, $50\text{ m}$ deck width) and the substructure pier grid ($4.0\text{ m} \times 4.0\text{ m}$ columns and beams). However:
> 1. The **50 m deck width must NOT be used as a 50 m hydraulic channel width**. The underlying channel is narrower.
> 2. The **4 m × 4 m column grid must NOT be used as hydraulic clear opening dimensions**. Clear width between piers is reduced by column thickness, and vertical headroom is unmeasured.
> 3. It can only be represented as a lumped resistance reach with regularized width and an elevated composite roughness ($n \ge 0.045$) accounting for form drag and debris choking.

### Question 4: Can the Defence Colony tributary be numerically modeled?
> **NO.**  
> NGT OA 6/2012 establishes that the tributary has a total length of $1,600\text{ m}$ ($1,300\text{ m}$ covered on columns and beams, $300\text{ m}$ open channel). However, no cross-sectional dimensions, invert levels, slope data, or flow hydrographs exist in the public domain. It must be treated as an uncharacterized lateral boundary inflow.

### Question 5: Do we have any authoritative surveyed invert profile?
> **NO.**  
> Neither municipal authorities (NDMC, MCD, PWD, I&FCD) nor tribunal filings provide a geodetic surveyed bed invert profile in the public domain. All current invert levels in the project are **`DERIVED SYNTHETIC OFFSETS`** ($Z_{\text{bank}} - H$) derived from the Copernicus DSM bank surface and carry an elevation uncertainty of $\pm 1.0\text{ m}$ to $\pm 1.5\text{ m}$.

### Question 6: Do we have any authoritative hydraulic clear-opening dimensions?
> **NO.**  
> No public authoritative document provides the internal net clear waterway width or vertical clear rise for either Reach 1 (Africa Avenue box culvert) or Reach 2 (Bus Depot covered reach and open channel). All available figures represent outer structural envelopes or regional master planning ranges.

### Question 7: What is the single most important remaining geometry datum?
> **The geodetic surveyed bed invert elevation profile (longitudinal L-section) and internal clear cross-section barrel dimensions (width × height / cell count) of the Africa Avenue box culvert (Reach 1) and the open Kushak channel (Reach 2).**  
> Without true surveyed bed levels and barrel geometry, energy slopes, backwater profiles, gravity flow limits, and surcharge thresholds cannot be definitively validated.

### Question 8: Is there any remaining public acquisition route worth pursuing before we begin hydraulic coding?
> **NO public, unauthenticated download routes remain.**  
> Every available open public route — NGT judicial filings, UTTIPEC master plan records, open government tender notices, satellite DSMs, and academic studies — has been comprehensively audited and exhausted. The detailed engineering drawings and AutoCAD files commissioned under the 2020 NDMC Topographical Survey and referenced in NIT No. 52/EE(R-III)/2025-26 are securely restricted behind authenticated Class-III DSC bidder logins on the Delhi Government e-procurement portal.  
> Further delay in search of public open data will produce zero new geometry. Hydraulic modeling must therefore proceed using the explicit, regularized parameter envelopes and comprehensive uncertainty sensitivity analyses documented herein.
