## 4. QUALITY CONTROL & AMBIGUITY AVOIDANCE NOTES

* **Anti-Ambiguity Strategy:** The request does not assert as fact that the survey was executed or completed. Phrasing explicitly requests: *"records relating to the survey/procurement and, if completed, the resulting deliverables."* This prevents summary dismissal on the grounds that the applicant made an incorrect assertion.
* **Separation of Procurement vs Deliverables:** If the survey was delayed, abandoned, or never executed post-NIQ, Part A forces disclosure of the cancellation/non-execution record, while Part B requests deliverables conditional upon completion.
* **Native Digital Format Emphasis:** Public authorities often convert vector CAD drawings into degraded black-and-white A4 PDFs where elevation figures are illegible. The explicit invocation of Section 7(9) mandates supply in native format (.dwg/.dxf/.csv) if held in electronic form.\n
