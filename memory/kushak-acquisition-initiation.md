**Basis:** [[kushak-data-acquisition-spec.md]]

**Initiation:** Formal commencement of targeted geometry acquisition survey for Kushak Nallah hydraulic model upgrade to deterministic readiness.

**Responsible Parties:** Survey team (to be contracted/assigned)

**Timeline & Deliverables:**

1. **Longitudinal Profile Package** (Priority 1)
   - Duration: Week 1-2
   - Deliverable: Surveyed invert elevations at 20m intervals (0.000-5.028km) + hydraulic structures/breaks
   - Provenance Target: OBSERVED (GTS datum)
   - Uncertainty Target: <0.1m

2. **Structures & Connectivity Package** (Priority 2)
   - Duration: Week 2-3
   - Deliverable: Surveyed inlet/outlet inverts, crest levels, vent dimensions for all junction chambers, bridges, culverts, outfalls
   - Provenance Target: OBSERVED
   - Includes: Topological verification of node connections

3. **Cross-Section Package** (Priority 3)
   - Duration: Week 3-4
   - Deliverable: Total station survey of bottom width, bank heights, side slopes, material characterization at CS-01 through CS-07
   - Provenance Target: OBSERVED

4. **Africa Avenue Tunnel Package** (Priority 4)
   - Duration: Week 4-5
   - Deliverable: Internal width/height, invert/soffit elevations at 50m intervals via CCTV/inspection of subsurface conduit (Way 44351567)
   - Provenance Target: OBSERVED

**Objective:** Achieve OBSERVED/OFFICIAL provenance for all critical physical geometry parameters to resolve Phase 3J P0 geometric blockers and enable deterministic hydraulic network assembly for Phase 4 progression.
